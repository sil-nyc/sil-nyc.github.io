    Why this lab

    We design spaces every day that change how people feel, think and behave. The research that explains how already exists, but it rarely reaches the people drawing the buildings.

    The lab closes that gap. One question at a time, a small group turns the evidence into a framework any architect can use, and we publish it openly.

    Aashna BajajFounding Researcher, Spatial Intelligence Lab

    How a sprint works

    Each sprint is one question, a small group and eight working weeks, starting once the group is formed. It follows the same shape every time.

    A

    Topic

    One question about how space affects the mind and body, chosen by vote.

    B

    Goal

    Turn the research on that question into guidance architects can use in a real project.

    C

    Expert

    Two private conversations with an expert in the field: one to guide the research, one to review the draft.

    D

    Result

    A design framework, each recommendation graded by the strength of its evidence. Published free.

    Where topics come from

    Every topic starts as an idea from inside the lab.

    1. 1

      Idea bank

      Working group members add questions, any time during a sprint.

    2. 2

      Shortlist

      At the end of the sprint, three are shortlisted.

    3. 3

      Vote

      Everyone who applies to the next sprint votes.

    4. 4

      Chosen

      The winner becomes the next topic. The others stay in the bank.
